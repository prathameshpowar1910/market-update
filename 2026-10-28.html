IN Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 259.48 ▲ +2.28 (+0.89%) Prev close 257.20

Indian equities delivered a strong performance on Wednesday, with the headline NIFTY 50 gaining 0.89% to close at 259.48. Market movement was heavily anchored by index heavyweights, notably Reliance Industries (+1.01%), Infosys (+0.91%), and Axis Bank (+0.90%). The Nifty Bank sector matched the broader index's gain (+0.89%), providing essential structural support despite mild profit-taking in market leaders like HDFC Bank (-0.56%) and Bajaj Finance (-0.76%).

Corporate earnings season injected strong stock-specific momentum across sectors. Steel Authority of India (SAIL) made headlines with a massive four-fold increase in Q2 net profit to Rs 1,750 crore on higher revenues. Similarly, United Breweries saw profits nearly triple to Rs 134 crore, benefiting from strong volume growth and consumer shift toward premium offerings. While defensive sectors like FMCG, Auto, and IT remained largely flat overall, the risk-on tone kept market breadth favorable.
