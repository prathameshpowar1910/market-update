IN Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 258.98 ▼ -0.69 (-0.27%) Prev close 259.67

Indian markets ended slightly lower on Saturday as the NIFTY 50 fell 0.27% to close at 258.98. The downturn was largely driven by sharp losses in index heavyweights Reliance Industries (-2.59%) and HDFC Bank (-1.85%), alongside technology majors Infosys (-1.40%) and TCS (-0.95%). Despite overall index pressure, stock-specific action remained resilient on the back of strong corporate earnings reports.

Financials showed a sharp divergence. Non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) outshined the broader market, with Bajaj Finance rising 1.61% and Shriram Finance posting a impressive 59% year-on-year surge in Q2 net profit to Rs 3,677 crore. ICICI Bank (+0.71%) also offered buffer against HDFC Bank's slide. Outside of banking, strong operating metrics were seen in metals and auto, highlighted by Jindal Steel's 21% profit gain and Tata Motors' robust commercial vehicle profitability.

Looking ahead, surging international crude oil prices (+4.31%) pose an immediate margin threat to state refiners like BPCL, which reported a 21% drop in Q2 profit despite top-line revenue growing 29%. Investors remain cautious as elevated energy prices and sticky global interest rates keep broad upside capped.
