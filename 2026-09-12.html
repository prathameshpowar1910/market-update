IN Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 267.15 ▼ -2.93 (-1.08%) Prev close 270.08

Indian equity markets faced notable selling pressure, with the Nifty 50 dropping 1.08%. The downturn was driven primarily by weakness in IT majors like HCLTech (-2.43%) and Wipro (-0.81%), alongside heavyweights Reliance Industries and ICICI Bank. Market sentiment was further dampened by domestic liquidity management actions after the Reserve Bank of India stepped in to drain Rs 1 lakh crore due to undersubscribed Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions.

Despite the broader decline, private banking giants showed sharp divergence. Axis Bank (+1.65%) and HDFC Bank (+0.98%) led the gainers, though Nifty Bank ultimately closed 1.08% lower. Primary market activity remained vibrant as NSE adjusted its upcoming IPO parameters by reducing offer size and lowering the price band, while Granules India saw promoter block deals worth Rs 1,500 crore picked up by global institutions like Goldman Sachs and BNP Paribas.
