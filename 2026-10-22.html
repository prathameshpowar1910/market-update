The Indian equity market experienced a difficult trading session on Thursday, with the benchmark NIFTY 50 falling 0.98% to close at 261.30. Heavy selling pressure across core index heavyweights pulled sentiment lower, particularly within the financial and technology sectors. Sectoral performance saw both Nifty Bank and Nifty IT decline significantly, down 0.98% and 0.82% respectively, while defensive and cyclical sectors like Auto, FMCG, Pharma, and Metals ended flat.

Strong stock-specific corporate updates, such as Mahindra Finance's 55% jump in Q2 net profit and Persistent Systems' new QIP launch, failed to broader market sentiment.
