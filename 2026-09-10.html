Indian equities traded on a weak note during Thursday's session, with the benchmark Nifty 50 declining 0.35% to close at 270.08. Sentiment was heavily impacted by macroeconomic headwinds originating abroad, particularly rising geopolitical friction in West Asia that propelled crude oil prices higher. As a major energy importer, elevated oil prices stoke concerns about India's trade deficit and imported inflation, pressuring investor risk appetite.

Selling pressure was concentrated in major financial and technology heavyweights. Heavyweight IT service providers Wipro (-3.00%) and TCS (-1.78%) experienced sharp declines alongside major lenders ICICI Bank (-2.00%) and Axis Bank (-1.72%).
