Indian equities edged higher in Saturday's session, with the Nifty 50 advancing 0.40% to close at 265.99. On the macroeconomic front, domestic markets remained resilient despite the Indian Rupee depreciating to 95.86 against the US Dollar and ongoing global yield uncertainty.
