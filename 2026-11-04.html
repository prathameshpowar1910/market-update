Indian markets recorded a strong trading session today, with the Nifty 50 climbing 1.64%. The rally was overwhelmingly driven by financial heavyweights, as the Nifty Bank index matched the benchmark's 1.64% rise. Private sector banking leaders HDFC Bank (+3.62%), ICICI Bank (+2.80%), and Axis Bank (+1.82%) spearheaded the upward momentum, reflecting robust institutional buying in high-conviction financial names.

Sectoral breath remained narrow, however, as defensive sectors including FMCG, Pharma, Auto, and Metals closed flat. The Nifty IT index gained 1.18% overall, supported by gains in Wipro (+0.66%), though Infosys bucked the trend to fall 1.46%. On the regulatory front, market regulator SEBI issued a ₹40 lakh penalty regarding the Axis Mutual Fund front-running case, highlighting continued regulatory scrutiny over fund manager integrity.
