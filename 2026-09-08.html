🇮🇳 Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 271.02 ▼ -2.29 (-0.84%)

Indian equity benchmarks continued their downward momentum on Tuesday, with the Nifty 50 dropping 0.84% as persistent selling pressure hit heavyweight constituents. Severe drag from top-tier technology firms—including Infosys (-3.81%), Wipro (-2.09%), and TCS (-1.28%)—alongside index heavyweight Reliance Industries (-0.95%) kept the broader market firmly in the red.

Despite the overarching bearish stance, selective buying was evident across specific themes. Life insurance providers such as LIC and HDFC Life rose up to 3% following impressive data showing a 33% year-over-year surge in August new business premiums. Additionally, defence stocks saw renewed buying interest following strong export figures, while capital goods stock GE Vernova jumped 8% after becoming the lowest bidder for a major Power Grid contract.

🌍 Global Markets

S&P 500 770.19 ▼ -2.98 (-0.39%)
