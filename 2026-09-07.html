The Indian equity market started the week on a positive note, with the benchmark Nifty 50 climbing 0.30%. Gains were largely driven by index heavyweights, led by Reliance Industries (+1.61%) alongside financial majors Bajaj Finance (+1.47%), HDFC Bank (+1.30%), and Axis Bank (+0.54%). However, overall market participation was concentrated, with key defensive sectors like FMCG, Pharma, and Metals ending completely flat (0.00%).

Sectorally, Nifty Bank (+0.30%) matched the benchmark's gains, propelled by strength in private lenders despite a minor dip in ICICI Bank (-0.14%). Meanwhile, Nifty IT gained 0.24% in aggregate, though individual stock performance diverged sharply; gains in Infosys (+0.18%) were offset by profit-booking in Tata Consultancy Services (-1.88%) and HCL Technologies (-1.24%).

Macroeconomic liquidity conditions showed positive signals as Reserve Bank of India (RBI) policy relaxations enabled Indian corporate entities to mobilize $7.70 billion through overseas debt markets. Market sentiment was also aided by regulatory relief after SEBI extended compliance deadlines for angel funds regarding accredited investor requirements.
