🇮🇳 Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 273.31 ▲ +0.81 (+0.30%)

Indian benchmark indices edged higher today, with the NIFTY 50 rising 0.30% to close at 273.31 points. Market gains were largely anchored by heavyweights in energy and banking, led by Reliance Industries (+1.61%), Bajaj Finance (+1.47%), and HDFC Bank (+1.30%). Financial stocks provided strong support as Nifty Bank advanced 0.30%, reflecting healthy domestic credit demand and positive sentiment around corporate financing.

However, broad market momentum was capped by sharp drag in select IT majors. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) tumbled 1.88% and HCL Tech fell 1.24%, even as Infosys managed a modest 0.18% gain. Meanwhile, regulatory tailwinds from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) have empowered domestic enterprises to tap international capital markets, driving overseas borrowing to $7.70 billion. Primary market interest also stayed firm as Prasol Chemicals secured Rs 150 crore from anchor investors ahead of its IPO.
