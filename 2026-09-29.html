IN Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 260.55 ▼ -3.63 (-1.37%) Prev close 264.18

Indian equity benchmark NIFTY 50 experienced a sharp sell-off, dropping 1.37% to 260.55 as broad-based profit taking gripped domestic heavyweights. Market drag was prominently driven by engineering conglomerate Larsen & Toubro (-2.81%), alongside core banking giants HDFC Bank (-2.30%) and ICICI Bank (-1.90%), and energy index heavyweight Reliance Industries (-2.24%). Concerns over elevated global oil prices and hardening yields overseas spurred risk aversion among domestic investors.

The banking sector bore the heaviest impact, dragging the Nifty Bank index down by 1.37%. In contrast, defensive resilience was visible in technology equities, where Nifty IT recorded a modest gain of 0.13%, supported by Infosys (+0.20%). On the corporate regulatory front, market regulator SEBI settled a case involving Vinod Adani and four Adani group companies regarding minimum public shareholding norms, bringing regulatory closure to the long-standing inquiry.
