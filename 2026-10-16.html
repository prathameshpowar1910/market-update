Indian benchmark indices closed lower on Friday, with the Nifty 50 declining by 0.56%. Weakness was heavily concentrated in the banking and financial services sector, where major heavyweights like Bajaj Finance (-2.55%), ICICI Bank (-1.54%), and HDFC Bank (-0.49%) faced persistent selling pressure. In contrast, large-cap IT stocks provided a cushion against further downside. Market leaders Infosys (+2.51%), TCS (+2.49%), and Wipro (+1.58%) attracted strong dip-buying interest following recent tech volatility. Meanwhile, regulatory updates kept market structure in focus as SEBI announced revised rules for Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) and derivative expiry settlement methodology, alongside proposals to integrate insurance details for retail investors.
