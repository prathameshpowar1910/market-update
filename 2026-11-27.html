Indian equities closed higher on Friday, with the Nifty 50 advancing 0.40% driven by a massive sector surge in technology. The Nifty IT index led gains with a 1.59% surge, as heavyweights HCL Technologies (+6.28%), TCS (+4.97%), Infosys (+4.70%), and Wipro (+4.13%) saw strong buying interest from investors seeking growth in export-oriented tech giants.

However, overall market gains were moderated by divergent trends within the financial sector. Macroeconomic concerns also loomed as Brent crude climbed past $95 per barrel, triggering selling pressure in Indian government bonds due to renewed food and energy inflation risks flagged by the Reserve Bank of India.
