Indian equity markets closed lower on Friday, with the Nifty 50 declining 0.50% to 257.63 amid global headwinds and domestic sector weakness. The technology basket led the sell-off as blue chips like Infosys (-2.06%), HCLTech (-1.52%), and Wipro (-1.30%) faced pressure following TCS earnings commentary and news of tightening US foreign worker program policies. Industrial heavyweights also dragged on sentiment, with Larsen & Toubro dropping 1.96% and Reliance Industries sliding 1.01%.

Despite the overarching weakness, banking stocks offered defensive support. ICICI Bank emerged as the top gainers' leader, rising 1.15%, alongside Bajaj Finance (+0.24%). Outside the main indices, market sentiment was rattled by institutional reshuffling, highlighted by GQG executing a massive Rs 9,395 crore block deal to exit its position in ITC after a prolonged stock decline.
