IN Indian Markets

NIFTY 50 272.68 ▲ +2.61 (+0.97%) Prev close 270.07

Indian equities posted strong gains on Thursday, with the NIFTY 50 advancing 0.97% to close up 272.68 points. The domestic rally was primarily driven by a dramatic sell-off in international crude oil prices, which sank over 8% following the US midterm election results. Lower oil prices provide significant macroeconomic relief to India's import-heavy economy, dampening headline inflation expectations and reducing the current account deficit.

The broader market momentum lifted financial heavyweights like Bajaj Finance (+2.38%) and Reliance Industries (+0.46%), while Indian sovereign bonds staged their biggest rally since April. However, top IT blue-chips TCS (-2.70%) and Infosys (-1.65%) faced targeted selling pressure despite overall stability in technology sector indices, highlighting selective profit-booking among large-cap tech exporters following political updates in key Western markets.
